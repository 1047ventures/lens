# Lens privacy policy

Lens is a Mac app for running a small creative business. This page explains what it does with your Google data.

- **What Lens reads:** when you sign in with Google, Lens can read your Gmail, Google Drive, Sheets, Docs and Calendar so it can find inquiries, attach emails and files to your projects, and spot calendar clashes. It can write email drafts that you review and send yourself.
- **Where it goes:** your data stays on your Mac. Lens has no server. Your Google sign-in is kept in your Mac's Keychain.
- **AI features:** if you use Ask Lens, the parts of your data needed to answer a question are sent to Anthropic's Claude API using your own API key, under Anthropic's terms. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.
- **What Lens never does:** send email on its own, delete mail, change your calendar, or see your Google password.
- **Stop at any time:** click Disconnect in Lens, or remove Lens at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Lens's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
